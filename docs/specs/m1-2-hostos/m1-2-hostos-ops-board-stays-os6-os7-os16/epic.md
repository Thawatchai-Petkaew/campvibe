# M1.2 · HostOS — Ops Board & Stays (OS6, OS7, OS16) (CAM-290)

## Why

Most Thai camps sell most of their nights over LINE, by phone, or at the gate. None of that reaches CampVibe today, so the availability a camper sees is systematically too optimistic and the host's own calendar is missing the stays they actually took. This epic gives a host one register that the platform's capacity math already reads. **KPI:** host-recorded stays per week (no baseline exists — instrument first, target after 4 weeks); guardrail: double-booking = 0, and no camper-facing number moves for reasons other than a real stay.

## Scope

**In**

- Record a stay that did not come through the site (walk-in / LINE / phone), counted by the same availability functions as every other booking — CAM-313 (**the first HostOS slice**, per [ADR-017](../../../adr/ADR-017-host-recorded-stay-and-booking-discriminator.md); re-scoped 2026-08-06 to drop its dependency on `HostLead`/`Quote`/`DepositRecord`, none of which exist)
- Check-in / check-out / no-show with real guard rules and an audit trail — CAM-64. This is CAM-290's stated exit criterion and it needs three things that do not exist: a `NO_SHOW` status, a validated transition table, and an `AuditLog` row per change. `app/api/bookings/[id]/route.ts:89-92` is currently a bare update, so `CANCELLED → PENDING` and a jump straight to `COMPLETED` are both permitted today — for **camper** bookings too. Fixing it here closes a shipped defect, not just a HostOS gap.
- Host booking detail surfacing the guest's special requests — CAM-63
- Map view of daily stay status across the camp (OS6) — CAM-316
- Day passes for day-use guests, separate from overnight stays (OS16) — CAM-317

**Out**

- Leads, quotes and deposits → epic CAM-289 (HostOS · Leads & Quotes). The first slice deliberately does **not** wait for them; `Booking.leadId` and `DepositRecord.bookingId` arrive later as additive nullable FKs.
- Editing a recorded stay after creation (moving dates re-opens the capacity race and needs its own transaction design) → follow-up story in this epic
- Filtering the stay list by `วันนี้` / `พรุ่งนี้` / a date range → follow-up story in this epic (dropped from CAM-313 v2 to keep that PR under ~400 lines)
- Linking a host-recorded stay to a camper account that appears later, and any cross-visit guest profile → ADR-012 §7, still deferred
- POS, rental, daily close → epics CAM-291 and CAM-292

## Build order inside this epic

CAM-313 → CAM-64 → (CAM-63 · the day filter · edit) → CAM-316 → CAM-317. CAM-313 comes first because every other story here needs a stay row to act on, and because it is the only one that changes a camper-visible number on its own. Cross-epic sequencing (where CAM-289's lead/quote/deposit stories fit) lives in `../feature.md` § Sequencing.
